## 🌍 The Problem

Electricity powers our everyday lives, but its environmental impact changes throughout the day.

While people often consider electricity costs, they may not realize that **using the same amount of electricity at different times can have different associated carbon emissions.**

Most households lack simple, accessible tools to make environmentally informed energy decisions.

## 💡 Our Solution

**EcoWatt AI** is an AI-powered sustainability initiative designed to help households make smarter electricity-consumption decisions.

By identifying potentially cleaner periods for electricity usage, EcoWatt aims to reduce electricity-related carbon footprints without significantly disrupting daily routines.

> **Same Energy. Smarter Timing. Greener Future.**

## ❓ Questions

### Isn't electricity the same throughout the day?

Electricity serves the same purpose, but the energy sources contributing to the grid and the environmental impact of consumption can change throughout the day.

### Does EcoWatt reduce electricity consumption?

Not necessarily. EcoWatt focuses on encouraging electricity usage during potentially cleaner periods.

### Why not always use electricity during the afternoon?

Afternoon electricity is not always cleaner. Environmental impact depends on grid conditions, electricity demand, and energy generation sources.

### Don't electricity companies already provide cheaper hours?

Many utilities publish time-of-use pricing. However, **electricity costs and environmental impact are not necessarily the same.**

### Are carbon emission reductions guaranteed?

No. Environmental benefits depend on actual grid conditions, forecasting accuracy, and user behavior.
